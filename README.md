# Waterfalls Pool Services LLC: sample site

A one-page sample site I put together for Waterfalls Pool Services LLC (McKinney, TX). It's a concept, not their official website, and it says so at the top of the page and in the footer.

Live preview: https://preview-waterfalls-pool.vercel.app

The request form doesn't send anything to a server. It just opens your own email app with a message addressed to waterfallspoolservice@gmail.com, and the phone buttons dial (214) 995-2979. There's no tracking or analytics, and the fonts are self-hosted.

## What's on the page

Only details from Waterfalls Pool Services LLC's own website and public listings: business name, McKinney / Collin County, phone, email, its headline line, and the plans and extra services listed on waterfallspoolservice.com. The rainy-winter note cites the NWS Fort Worth outlook. Anything I couldn't confirm is left out or marked to be confirmed. There are no reviews, ratings, prices, years in business or team photos.

## Indexing

The page has `noindex, nofollow` in a meta tag and in the `X-Robots-Tag` header (`vercel.json`), and `robots.txt` disallows everything, so it shouldn't turn up in search.
